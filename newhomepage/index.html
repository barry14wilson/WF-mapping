FAQ

Honest answers.

01Does the finder need to install an app?

No. Any phone camera can scan the code. The finder sees only what you've chosen to share and can message you straight away.

02Is my child's information safe?

You choose exactly what a finder sees, which can be as little as "please contact my parent". Your phone number and address are never shown, and you can edit or switch off a profile at any time. Children's profiles are always managed by a parent or guardian.

03What does a Medical ID band show when scanned?

Only what you choose: for example allergies, conditions, medication, blood type and emergency contacts. Update it in the app and the band shows the change straight away. Every scan notifies you and your guardians.

04Where does the safety rating come from?

Official police and government data. In England, Wales and Northern Ireland that's street-level data from data.police.uk, refreshed monthly. Scotland uses official data-zone statistics, and other countries use national and international sources. Ratings run from 1 (higher crime) to 5 (safest).

05Does SOS call the emergency services?

No. SOS alerts your own emergency contacts with your live location. In an emergency, always call 999 in the UK or 112 in the EU first.

06What if a tag is damaged or lost?

Each tag links to an address we control, so you can switch off a lost tag and link a new one to the same profile in seconds.

07Can schools, clubs or events order in bulk?

Yes. Event wristband packs, school safety packs and name labels are available, with codes you assign in the app.
