Give your kids a band or school label with a Wiley Fox code. Anyone who finds them can scan it, see only what you choose to share, and reach you straight away. No app, no phone number on show.

FAQ

Honest answers.

Does the finder need to install an app?

No. Any phone camera can scan the code. The finder lands on a private page showing only what you've chosen to share, and can message you straight away.

Is my child's information safe?

You choose exactly what a finder sees, and it can be as little as "please contact my parent". Your phone number and address are never shown, and you can edit or switch off a profile at any time. Children's profiles are always managed by a parent or guardian.

Where does the safety rating come from?

From official police and government crime data. In England, Wales and Northern Ireland that's street-level data from data.police.uk, refreshed monthly. Scotland uses official data-zone statistics, and other countries use national and international sources. Ratings run from 1 (higher crime) to 5 (safest).

Does SOS call the emergency services?

No. SOS alerts your own emergency contacts with your live location. In an emergency, always call 999 in the UK or 112 in the EU first.

What if a tag gets damaged or lost?

Each tag links to an address we control, so you can deactivate a lost tag and link a new one to the same profile in seconds.

Can schools, clubs or events order in bulk?

Yes. Bulk packs of wristbands, slap bands and school name labels are available, with codes you can assign in the app.

Can I cancel Pro any time?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like and keep your free features.
